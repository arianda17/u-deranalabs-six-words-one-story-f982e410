# Six Words, One Story — Entry

## Winning line

**He promised forever. She counted seconds.**

Word count: **6** (He, promised, forever., She, counted, seconds.)

## Losing variants

1. **Sold her ring. Bought a ticket.** — "a" is filler; strong turn but not as tight as the winner.
2. **Found him asleep. Left the house.** — "asleep" is too passive and ambiguous; "the" is filler.
3. **She packed his bags. He stayed.** — ambiguous who stays and who leaves; reader can't fill in cleanly.
4. **He said forever. She said nothing.** — "nothing" is too explicit; "said" repeats; too little left unsaid.
